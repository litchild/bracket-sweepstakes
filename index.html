More to know: rounds, points, and this prototype

This prototype begins with 64 teams after the opening games. Teams advance through the Round of 64, Round of 32, Sweet 16, Elite 8, Final Four, and championship.

Correct picks earn 10, 20, 40, 80, 160, and 320 points in those rounds. A perfect bracket earns 1,920 points. Changing a winner clears later picks that depend on that team.

Entry is free in the concept. The proposed prize is 50,000 RMB; the team is still choosing the prize rule. This demo uses the 2026 field and placeholder dates. Entries stay in your browser and results are simulated in Standings.
