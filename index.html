New to March Madness?

  1. 64 college teams. One loss and you’re out. Six rounds in about three weeks, ending with the Final Four and a championship game.
  2. Your bracket: pick all 63 winners before round one. Picks lock March 18.
  3. Each bracket (East, West, South, Midwest) seeds its 16 teams from 1 (strongest) to 16. When a weaker seed (bigger number) wins, it’s an upset.
  4. Correct picks score points, more in later rounds. Entry is free. Games stream free on Bilibili. Win 50,000 RMB.
  5. Don’t know the teams? fills it by region or Chinese players.

Home regions (东 南 西 北) show where a school is located. The four bracket tabs (East, West, South, Midwest) only set who plays whom.

Pick a region. Its teams beat every team from another region. When two teams from your region meet, or two teams from other regions, it’s a coin flip weighted by seed. Each region has Chinese ambassadors who show you around.

Pick the team you want to win it all. That team wins every game. The other teams with Chinese players beat everyone else, and when two of them meet, it’s a coin flip weighted by seed.
